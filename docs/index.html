About

Dr. Meng Li is a professor, department chair of Decision & Information Sciences, Endowed C.T. Bauer Chair of AI, and founding director of the Bauer Human-Centered AI Institute at the University of Houston. His work examines how artificial intelligence reshapes organizational decision-making, healthcare delivery, and operational systems.
